    Options

    Closing the window that holds your pinned tabs normally loses them. If another window is still open, Tabmoor brings them back there. Tabs inside tab groups are never moved or pinned.

    Tip: some sites change their address when they load (gmail.com becomes mail.google.com). Adding a page with the Tabmoor toolbar button while you're on it saves the exact address.
